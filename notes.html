Link another device

  1. On the other device, open www.ron.computer/notes.html. On an iPhone, add it to your Home Screen first (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and open it from there, because the Home Screen app keeps its own storage.
  2. Open Settings & sync, choose I already sync on another device, then tap Scan and point it at this code. Pasting the link works too.
  3. Enter your passphrase. Done.

The link is encrypted and only works together with your passphrase.

Scan the code
