        How it works

        • Tap the farm to drop crumbs for the ants, tap an ant to follow it, and catch the bugs that wander by. Pop it out to keep it floating on your desktop.
        • Foragers walk up to the surface and haul food back to the store chambers.
        • Diggers tunnel out new chambers. More nest means room for more ants.
        • Nurses help the queen lay and raise the brood faster. Every egg costs a little food.
        • Weather changes every few minutes and follows the seasons. Rain softens the soil for digging but keeps foragers home. Check the forecast and shift jobs to match.
        • Upgrades unlock one at a time as the colony grows, starting with Picnic crumbs. Once unlocked, they stay unlocked on every farm.
        • Guards fight off raiders. Each raid is announced a minute and a half ahead, so move ants to Guard on the job bar. Battle drills and barracks make every guard count for more, and rain keeps raiders away. Lose a fight and the raiders take food and brood, a few ants may fall, and the colony is shaken up for a while.
        • Chambers: tap one on the farm to choose what it is: a nursery, pantry, barracks, fungus garden or rest room.
        • Events pop up now and then with a choice to make. Leave one for three minutes and the cautious choice is made for you.
        • Bugs wander by now and then: ladybugs, butterflies and, at night, fireflies. Tap one for a boost. Golden ones are rare and strong.
        • The view follows the nest and pulls back as the colony digs. Zoom with the buttons, a pinch or ctrl + scroll, and tap an ant to follow it around.
        • The farm keeps going while you're away, for up to 8 hours.
        • Fly the nest once the colony has 120 ants: queens leave to start a fresh farm. This farm resets, but every queen makes all your ants 10% better for good, and waiting longer sends more queens.
        • On a phone, the buttons along the bottom open your jobs, upgrades, ants and stats, and the menu at the top right has the rest. Add it to your home screen to use it like an app.

        Move your farm

        Copy a save code on this device, then paste it into the ant farm on your other one. Loading a code replaces the farm on that device, so play on one at a time.
