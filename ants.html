          How it works

          • Tap the farm to drop crumbs for the ants, tap an ant to follow it, and catch the bugs that wander by. Pop it out to keep it floating on your desktop.
          • Foragers walk up to the surface and haul food back to the store chambers.
          • Diggers tunnel out new chambers. More nest means room for more ants.
          • Nurses help the queen lay and raise the brood faster. Every egg costs a little food.
          • Weather changes every few minutes and follows the seasons. Rain softens the soil for digging but keeps foragers home. Check the forecast and shift jobs to match.
          • Upgrades unlock one at a time as the colony grows, starting with Picnic crumbs. Once unlocked, they stay unlocked on every farm. Hover over one, or press and hold it, for the exact numbers.
          • Guards fight off raiders. Each raid is announced a minute and a half ahead, so move ants to Guard on the job bar. Your scouts say whether a raid looks weaker, even or stronger than your guards, but it's only a guess. The fight plays out at the entrance with a bar showing who's winning, green for your guards and red for the raider, and guards you send mid-fight still count. Battle drills and barracks make every guard count for more, but raiders size themselves up against them and come back bolder after every raid you beat, so keep an eye on each one. Rain keeps raiders away. Lose a fight and the raiders take a good share of your food and brood, some ants fall, and the colony is shaken up for a while. They never wipe out the colony, and while you're away the guards just hold the entrance, so losses are lighter.
          • Chambers: tap one on the farm to choose what it is: a nursery, pantry, barracks, fungus garden or rest room.
          • Events pop up now and then with a choice to make. Leave one for three minutes and the cautious choice is made for you.
          • Bugs wander by now and then: ladybugs, butterflies and, at night, fireflies. Tap one for a boost. Golden ones are rare and strong.
          • Creatures in the soil crawl through now and then, leaving a trail of loose grains: grubs, pill bugs, mole crickets, springtails and the odd cicada nymph. Tap one before it burrows away. Very rarely there's a golden scarab, a young queen, or amber with something ancient inside: there are 5 pieces to find, and each makes everything 3% better for good.
          • The view follows the nest and pulls back as the colony digs. Zoom with the buttons, a pinch or ctrl + scroll, and tap an ant to follow it around.
          • The farm keeps going while you're away, at a quarter of the speed, for up to 8 hours.
          • Past farms are listed in Stats, and the last dozen keep their nest: tap Visit to look around an old one while this farm keeps going.
          • Fly the nest: queens leave to start a fresh farm. This farm resets, but every queen makes all your ants 10% better for good. Each flight needs more ants and a better food than the last one, all the way up to birthday cake, and the better the food you've found, the more queens fly. The Fly card says what this one needs.
          • On a phone, the buttons along the bottom open your jobs, upgrades, ants and stats, and the menu at the top right has the rest. Add it to your home screen to use it like an app.

          Move your farm

          Copy a save code on this device, then paste it into the ant farm on your other one. Loading a code replaces the farm on that device, so play on one at a time.
